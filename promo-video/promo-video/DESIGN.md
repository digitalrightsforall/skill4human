# Skill4Human: Rebooting Know-How - Design Spec

## Style Prompt
A modern, clean, and practical presentation of a tool that empowers. Light, airy canvas with sharp typography and interface elements. The mood is clear, optimistic, and highly legible, using the project's brand colors to highlight actionable insights.

## Colors
- **Primary**: #5e40e0 (Trusting Purple)
- **Tertiary-container**: #fdd400 (Actionable Yellow for "Heuristics")
- **Background**: #f1f4f7 (Clean Canvas)
- **Surface**: #ffffff (Content Cards)
- **Accent**: #eaeef3 (Section Separation)

## Typography
- **Headline**: Plus Jakarta Sans, Weight 700.
- **Body**: Plus Jakarta Sans, Weight 400.

## What NOT to Do
- No complex 3D; stick to clean 2D UI metaphors.
- No heavy shadows; use subtle elevation or border-based separation.
- No distracting motion; keep animations functional and focused on the content hierarchy.
